Find a business case template

Before you start

  • There are different business case templates depending on your proposal. Whole life cost is the main factor in determining what business case template you'll need and what approvals will apply.
  • Business cases should be a collaborative effort! Before you start drafting, you should involve your Finance Business Partner and a Commercial colleague to let them know what you are doing. Depending on your proposal, you may also need to engage and seek assurance from other Subject Matter Experts.
  • Plan enough time for assurance and approvals - depending on what you're asking for, the business case process can take 12+ weeks.

If your proposal is over £2m and you're unsure which template to use, speak to your Finance Business Partner or a Commercial colleague first to identify the correct template. You can then come back to this service to access the template.

For proposals under £2m, this service will help you find the right template.

What to expect using this service

We will ask you a few questions, including:

  • the total value of your business case
  • whether the spend is part of an existing project or programme
  • whether it's novel, contentious, sets precedent or repercussive

Based on your answers, you'll understand which template you need, who to speak to in order to start drafting your business case and what approvals will apply.
