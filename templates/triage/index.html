What to expect from this service

Use this service to find the right business case route and template for your proposal.

You'll be asked a short set of questions about your proposal, including:

  • estimated whole life cost
  • type of spend
  • anything that may affect assurance or approval

This should take around 5 to 8 minutes.

At the end, this service will:

  • tell you whether you need a business case
  • identify the business case route you need to follow
  • provide the correct business case template to download in Microsoft Word
  • explain who may need to review or approve your business case
  • signpost you to any extra guidance, assurance or support you may need
  • let you upload your approved business case so it can be logged centrally

Before you start the business case process

Before completing a business case, speak to your Finance Business Partner (FBP) or a Commercial colleague about your proposal.

Depending on your proposal, you may also need advice, assurance or approval from other Subject Matter Experts (SMEs). Your FBP or Commercial colleague can help you identify who else you need to involve.

Allow enough time for assurance and approvals. In some cases, the business case process can take 16 weeks or longer.

If your proposal is over £2 million and you are not sure which template you need, speak to the Investment Sub-Committee (ISC) Secretariat, your FBP or a Commercial colleague before using this service. You can then return to this service to access the right template.

If your proposal is under £2 million, this service can help you find the correct template.
