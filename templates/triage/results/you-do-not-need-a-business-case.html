You don't need a business case.

Because the whole life cost is less than £12,000 and is not part of an existing project or programme, a business case isn’t required.  

Depending on what you want to spend money on, you may still need to follow certain approval processes.

If your costs increase to over £12k (incl. VAT), you'll need to return to this service as your result will differ.

What to do next:

If you want to buy something of low value (between £1,000 and £12,000 incl VAT):

  • You need to follow the Low Value Purchase process. This can require setting up a supplier on SAP and gathering additional quotes from alternative suppliers.
  • For guidance on the steps to do this, visit the Finance Knowledge Centre

If you want to procure consultancy and professional services (C&PS):

  • If your spend is over £10,000 for C&PS, you may need to speak to the team. Further guidance and next steps can be found on the intranet here.

If you want to hire someone:

  • People, Capabilities & Change can help you. Contact them here

If you need external legal advice:

  • You need to get agreement from Government Legal Department (GLD) . This is due to HM Treasury rules around legal spend that we have to follow as a department.
  • You should also contact your Finance Business Partner.

If you need to commission research:

  • Contact Research Gateway.
  • You will need to fill in a proposal if the commission research and evaluation work is over £9,999 (excl. VAT).
  • You can find the template on the Research Gateway Intranet in the section "Projects over £9,999"

If this involves digital spend:

  • Contact Digital Front Door (DFD). They are the starting point as they run spend controls on behalf of Government Digital Service (GDS) and can advise whether you need further approvals, such as Pipeline Assurance Board.

Find everything about business cases at MHCLG on the Project Delivery Hub
