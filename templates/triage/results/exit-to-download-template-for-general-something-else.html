Before you start drafting

1. Speak to the right people early

Business cases are developed collaboratively. Contact the people who will advise on, review and approve your proposal as early as possible. You should contact:

  • Finance business partner (FBP)
    Explain your proposal and the total funding requested. Your Finance Business Partner will act as your Lead Subject Matter Expert (SME). They will:
    • confirm funding is available
    • assess the proposal against Accounting Officer standards
    • help you develop your business case
    • identify any additional approvals or assurance requirements
    • provide a recommendation on whether the proposal should proceed
  • Senior Responsible Owner (SRO) or Senior Civil Servant (SCS) Speak to the person with delegated authority to approve this spend. Let them know about your proposal early and that you will be seeking their approval.
  • Digital Front Door (DFD) If your proposal involves digital services, software, platforms, systems, technology or data, contact the Digital Front Door team. They oversee digital spend across the department and will advise on any assurance requirements. Depending on the nature and value of your proposal, you may also need to attend the Digital Pipeline Assurance Board. If this applies, the DFD team will arrange this.

2. Allow enough time

Additional approvals and assurance activities may be required depending on your proposal.

The end-to-end process can take 16 weeks or longer, so engage the right people early. They can help you understand the requirements, identify any additional Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) you need to involve and avoid delays later in the process.

3. Use supporting guidance

Review the available guidance before drafting your business case.

This will help you understand the information reviewers and approvers expect to see.

    Who approves my business case?

    After receiving assurance sign-off from your Finance Business Partner (FBP) and any additional Subject Matter Experts (SMEs), you will need approval from your Senior Responsible Owner (SRO) or Senior Civil Servant (SCS).

    Additional approvals

    Depending on the nature and value of your proposal, additional approvals may also be required.

    These may include:

    • Digital assurance
    • Consultancy and Professional Services (C&PS)
    • External legal advice
    • Investment Sub-Committee (ISC)
    • Other approval boards relevant to your proposal

    Your Finance Business Partner will help you identify any additional approvals that apply.
