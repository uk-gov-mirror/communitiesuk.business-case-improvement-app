You need to follow the Research Gateway process.

Based on your answers, this proposal must be submitted through the Research Gateway process before any spend can be approved.

The research gateway is designed to make sure large research projects are considered in the wider context of departmental research and deliver value for money. The process aims to provide challenge and scrutiny rather than block proposed projects.

What to do next

1. Before you start writing your business case, contact the Research Gateway team.

They will advise you on the process, confirm the appropriate route for your proposal, and support you in developing your business case.

2. Download the current Research Gateway template

Download the latest business case template from the Research Gateway intranet page. You can find it under the section heading “Research funding and approvals”.

Do not use a template from a previous business case or one shared by a colleague, as guidance and requirements may have changed.

Who will need to approve this?

You may be required to attend the Research Review Gateway Board as part of the approval process.

The Research Gateway team will explain any review and approval requirements and help you plan your work accordingly.
