You need to follow the Research Gateway process.

Based on your answers, your proposal must go through the Research Gateway process before any spend can be approved.

The Research Gateway helps make sure large research projects are considered alongside other departmental research activity and deliver value for money. The process provides review and challenge to help strengthen proposals and support good decision-making.

What to do next

1. Contact the Research Gateway team

Before you start writing your business case, contact the Research Gateway team.

They can:

  • explain the process
  • confirm the correct route for your proposal
  • support you in developing your business case

2. Download the latest Research Gateway template

Download the latest business case template from the Research Gateway intranet page.

Always use the latest version of the template. Do not use a template from a previous business case or one shared by a colleague, as requirements may have changed.

Who will need to approve this?

You may be asked to present your proposal to the Research Review Gateway Board as part of the approval process.

The Research Gateway team will explain any review and approval requirements and help you plan your work accordingly.
