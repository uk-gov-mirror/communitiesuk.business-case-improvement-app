Before you start drafting

Help

1. Speak to the right people early

Business cases are developed collaboratively. Contact the people who will advise on, review and approve your proposal as early as possible. You should contact:

  • Commercial team Explain your proposal and the total funding requested, and let them know you need Subject Matter Expert (SME) support. They will assign a Lead SME to support the development of your business case and provide a recommendation on whether it should proceed.
  • Finance business partner (FBP)
    Explain your proposal and the total funding requested. They will:
    • confirm funding is available
    • assess the proposal against Accounting Officer standards
    • provide a recommendation on whether it should proceedt
  • Senior Responsible Owner (SRO) or Senior Civil Servant (SCS) Speak to the person with delegated authority to approve this spend. Let them know about your proposal early and that you will be seeking their approval.
  • Digital Front Door (DFD) If your proposal involves digital services, software, platforms, systems, technology or data, contact the Digital Front Door team. They oversee digital spend across the department and will advise on any assurance requirements. Depending on the nature and value of your proposal, you may also need to attend the Digital Pipeline Assurance Board. If this applies, the DFD team will arrange this.

2. Allow enough time

Additional approvals and assurance activities may be required depending on your proposal.

The end-to-end process can take 16 weeks or longer, so engage the right people early. They can help you understand the requirements, identify any additional Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) you need to involve and avoid delays later in the process.

3. Use supporting guidance

Review the available guidance before drafting your business case.

This will help you understand the information reviewers and approvers expect to see.

Access the 'Grant' guidance here.

4. Obtain assurance and approval

Your Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) must review and approve your business case.

Once it is complete, share the business case with the relevant approvers.

See Who approves my business case? below for more information.

5. Upload your approved business case

Once all required approvals have been received, return to this service and upload your approved business case.

You must upload it here so it can be recorded centrally.

Who approves my business case?

Help

After receiving assurance sign-off from your Lead SME and Finance Business Partner (FBP), you will need approval from your Senior Responsible Owner (SRO) or Senior Civil Servant (SCS).

Additional approvals

Depending on the nature and value of your proposal, additional approvals may also be required.

  • Resource DEL Administration (RDEL Admin)
    RDEL Admin is funding used for departmental running and administration costs.
    If your proposal includes £1 million or more of RDEL Admin spend, Investment Sub-Committee (ISC) approval may be required.
    Speak to your Finance Business Partner (FBP) if you are unsure whether this applies.
  • Consultancy and Professional Services (C&PS)
    Consultancy and Professional Services (C&PS) involves bringing in external expertise, specialist knowledge or a supplier to deliver a specific piece of work.
    If your proposal includes more than £10,000 of C&PS spend, additional approvals may be required.
    Refer to the relevant intranet guidance for more information.
  • External legal advice
    If your proposal requires external legal advice, approval may be required from the Government Legal Department (GLD) in line with HM Treasury requirements.

Depending on the nature of your proposal, additional approval boards may apply. Work with your Finance Business Partner and Procurement Lead SME to understand any additional approvals that may be needed.
