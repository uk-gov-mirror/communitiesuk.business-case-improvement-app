Before you start drafting

1. Engage the right people early

Business cases are developed collaboratively. Engage the people who will advise on, assure and approve your proposal as early as possible. You should contact:

  • The commercial team Explain your activity and your total requested amount. They will assign you corporate partner to support the development of your business case and provide a recommendation on whether it should proceed.

  • Digital Corporate Office at digitalbusinesscase@communities.gov.uk Make them aware of your proposal and the total value of the request. They oversee Digital Directorate budgets and will help coordinate the business case process, approvals and sign-off requirements.

  • Digital Front Door (DFD) If your proposal involves digital services, software, platforms, systems, technology or data, contact the Digital Front Door team. They oversee the department's digital spend and will advise on any assurance requirements. Depending on the value and nature of your proposal, you may also need to attend the Digital Pipeline Assurance Board. If this applies, the DFD team will arrange this for you.

2. Allow enough time

Depending on the nature of your proposal, additional approvals and assurance may be required. The end-to-end process can take 16+ weeks, so engage the Digital Corporate Office early to understand the requirements for your proposal and identify any additional subject matter experts you need to involve.

3. Use the supporting guidance

Review the available guidance before drafting your business case. This will help you understand the information reviewers and approvers expect to see. Access the 'Procurement' guidance here.

Who approves my business case

You will follow the Digital Directorate streamlined business case process.

Once your business case has been completed, it should be submitted to digitalbusinesscase@communities.gov.uk . The Digital Corporate Office will coordinate the review process and obtain the required approvals on your behalf. It will be signed off by Digital Directorate SRO/SCS.

Additional approvals may also be required depending on the nature and value of your proposal:

  • Resource DEL Administration (RDEL Admin)
    If your proposal includes £1 million or more of RDEL Admin spend, approval from the Investment Sub-Committee (ISC) may be required. Speak to your Finance Business Partner if you're unsure whether this applies.

  • Consultancy and Professional Services (C&PS)
    If your proposal includes more than £10,000 of Consultancy and Professional Services spend, additional approvals may be required. Refer to the relevant intranet guidance for further information.

  • External legal advice
    If your proposal requires external legal advice, approval may be required from the Government Legal Department (GLD) in line with HM Treasury requirements
