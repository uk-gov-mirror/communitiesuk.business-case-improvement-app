You need to do a Business Justification Case (BJC)

Based on your answers, you need to complete a Business Justification Case (BJC) before any spend can be approved.

This is because your proposal:

  1. is between £12,000 and £2 million, and
  2. is not novel, repercussive or contentious

Note: A Business Justification Case must relate to a single, standalone piece of spend. You should not use a BJC to:

  • manage changes to an existing approved business case
  • handle scope or funding changes within an ongoing project or programme

If your spend is part of a wider programme, you should speak with your Senior Responsible Officer.

There are specific templates for different business justification cases. Find the option below that best describes your spend for the appropriate template and guidance on what to do next.
