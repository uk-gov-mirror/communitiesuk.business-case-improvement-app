You don't need a business case.

Based on your answers, you do not need a business case because:

  • the whole life cost (the total cost of the proposal over its lifetime) is less than £12,000 (including VAT)
  • the request is not part of an existing project or programme with an approved business case

However, because your request involves digital activity, technology, data, systems or digital services, you must notify the Digital Front Door (DFD) team.

This helps MHCLG maintain visibility of digital activity and technology spend across the department, including requests that do not require a business case.

If the whole life cost increases above £12,000 (including VAT), you should return to this service, as your result may change.

What to do next

Email the Digital Front Door (DFD) team to let them know about your proposal.
