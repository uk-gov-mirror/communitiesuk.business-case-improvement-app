Before you start drafting

1. Engage the right people early

Business cases are developed collaboratively. Engage the people who will advise on, assure and approve your proposal as early as possible. You should contact:

  • HR Business Partners (HRBP) Explain your proposal and the total value of the request. They will assign a Corporate Partner to support the development of your business case and provide a recommendation on whether it should proceed.

  • Your finance business partner (FBP) Explain your proposal and the total value of the request. They will confirm funding is available, assess the proposal against Accounting Officer standards and provide a recommendation on whether it should proceed. Digital Directorate only: If you are seeking funding from the Digital budget, you do not contact your Finance Business Partner. Instead, contact the Digital Corporate Office at digitalbusinesscase@communities.gov.uk .

  • Your SRO/SCS in your area with the delegated authority to sign off this spend - advise them of what you're doing early and that you will be seeking their approval soon.

  • Digital Front Door (DFD) If your proposal involves digital services, software, platforms, systems, technology or data, contact the Digital Front Door team. They oversee the department's digital spend and will advise on any assurance requirements. Depending on the value and nature of your proposal, you may also need to attend the Digital Pipeline Assurance Board. If this applies, the DFD team will arrange this for you.

2. Allow enough time

Depending on the nature of your proposal, additional approvals and assurance may be required. The end-to-end process can take 16+ weeks, so engage the right people early - they will help guide you through additional requirements.

3. Use the supporting guidance

Review the available guidance before drafting your business case. This will help you understand the information reviewers and approvers expect to see. Access the Contingent Labour Step-by-Step guide.pptx

Who approves my business case?

After securing assurance sign off from your Procurement Lead SME and Finance Business Partner, you will need to ask your area SRO or SCS to approve this.

Additional approvals may also be required depending on the nature and value of your proposal:

  • Resource DEL Administration (RDEL Admin)
    If your proposal includes £1 million or more of RDEL Admin spend, approval from the Investment Sub-Committee (ISC) may be required. Speak to your Finance Business Partner if you're unsure whether this applies.

  • Consultancy and Professional Services (C&PS)
    If your proposal includes more than £10,000 of Consultancy and Professional Services spend, additional approvals may be required. Refer to the relevant intranet guidance for further information.

  • External legal advice
    If your proposal requires external legal advice, approval may be required from the Government Legal Department (GLD) in line with HM Treasury requirements.
