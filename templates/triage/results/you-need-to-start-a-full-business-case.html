You need to engage with the 3-stage business case process.

Based on your answers, it sounds like you need to engage with the 3-stage business case.

Depending on what stage your proposal is at, the template you'll need will either be the Project Brief, Strategic Outline Case (SOC), Outline Business Case (OBC) or the Full Business Case.

Avoid using an old template from a previous business case or one given to you by a colleague.

Any significant changes to scope, cost and/or time of a project or programme needs consultation with Investment Sub-Committee (ISC) Secretariat and your Finance Business Partner (FBP) to consider if reapproval at ISC is required.

What to do next

If you know the template you need:

  • Engage with your SMEs as early as possible.
  • Download your template from the template library

If you don't know the template you need:

  • Engage with your SMEs as early as possible. They can help you.
  • Visit the stage section on the Project Delivery Hub to see which stage your proposal fits in.
  • If you're still unsure, contact the ISC Secretariat at ISCSecretariat@communities.gov.uk. They can help you identify which template to start with and how to book an ISC slot for approval.
