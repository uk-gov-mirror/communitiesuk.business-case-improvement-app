You don't need a business case.

Based on your answers, a business case is not required because:

  • the whole life cost is less than £12,000 (including VAT), and
  • the request is not part of an existing project or programme with an approved business case

However, because your request involves digital activity, technology, data, systems or digital services, you must notify the Digital Front Door (DFD) team.

This helps the department maintain visibility of digital activity and technology spend across MHCLG, including requests that do not require a business case.

If your costs increase above £12,000 (including VAT), you'll need to return to this service as your result may differ.

What to do next:

Email the Digital Front Door team to let them know of your proposal.
