Before you start drafting

1. Engage the right people early

Business cases are developed collaboratively. Engage the people who will advise on, assure and approve your proposal as early as possible. You should contact:

  • Your finance business partner (FBP) Explain your activity and your total requested amount. They will check it is in budget and passes accounting officer tests. They will also need to provide a recommendation to proceed.

  • Your SRO/SCS in your area with the delegated authority to sign off this spend - advise them of what you're doing early and that you will be seeking their approval soon.

  • Digital Front Door (DFD) If your proposal involves digital services, software, platforms, systems, technology or data, contact the Digital Front Door team. They oversee the department's digital spend and will advise on any assurance requirements. Depending on the value and nature of your proposal, you may also need to attend the Digital Pipeline Assurance Board. If this applies, the DFD team will arrange this for you.

2. Allow enough time

Depending on the nature of your proposal, additional approvals and assurance may be required. The end-to-end process can take 16+ weeks, so engage the right people early - they will help guide you through additional requirements.
